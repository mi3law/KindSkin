    Measurement framework · CDLQI

    The Children's Dermatology Life Quality Index (CDLQI) is a validated questionnaire measuring how skin conditions affect young people's lives. KindSkin's emotional and social categories are informed by the life-quality domains it explores (symptoms, feelings, school, leisure, relationships, sleep) — KindSkin does not reproduce, administer, or score the CDLQI.

    Data & privacy

    • ●Every check-in stays in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded, shared, or sold.
    • ●No accounts, no analytics, no advertising.
    • ●Clearing browser data or tapping Reset all erases history permanently — export first if you'd like a copy.
    • ●Demo data is synthetic and clearly labelled; it is never research data.

    ⚠ Important disclaimer — not medical advice

    KindSkin is a student research prototype. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT diagnose, treat, or provide clinical, dermatological, or psychological advice. Forecasts and insights are illustrative estimates based only on your own self-reported logs and may be wrong. Always talk with a parent or guardian and a qualified healthcare professional — such as a dermatologist, pediatrician, or licensed mental-health professional — about your skin and your wellbeing.

    If you're feeling overwhelmed, hopeless, or unsafe, please reach out to a trusted adult right away. In the US you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time; elsewhere, contact your local emergency number.
